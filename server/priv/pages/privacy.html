Privacy Policy

Fazoura Party · Last updated 23 September 2026

The short version. Fazoura Party has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You don't give us your name, email or phone number, and we don't ask. A game lives in the server's memory while it is being played and is gone when the room closes. The only things we keep are quizzes that someone asked us to publish, and reports people send us about them.

This policy covers the Fazoura Party app for Android, the web app, and the server they connect to (together, “the service”). The service is run by Anis Abdellatif (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about it, write to contact@anisabdellatif.me.

What stays on your device

These are stored by the app on your phone or in your browser, and never sent to us unless described below:

  • Private quizzes you create or save, including their photos.
  • A publisher key — a random value the app generates once. It is how the server recognises that a public quiz was published from your device, so that only you can change or remove it. It is not linked to who you are.
  • Room tokens for games you recently joined, so you can get back into a game if your connection drops. They are forgotten when the room closes.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes all of this.

Playing a game

To join a game you enter a display name. It can be anything; it doesn't need to be your real name. While a game is running, the server holds in memory:

  • the display names of the players and a colour assigned to each,
  • the answers each player submits and the scores that result,
  • the questions being played.

Everyone in the room sees each player's display name and score. After each question, the host and the players see the answers that were given. None of this is written to a database: it is deleted when the room closes, which happens shortly after everyone leaves or a few minutes after the game ends, and whenever the server restarts.

When a private quiz is hosted, the app sends it — photos included — to the server with the request to open the room. It is held in memory for as long as that room exists and is never stored.

Local network games

When a game is hosted on a local network, the players' devices talk directly to the host's device. Nothing about that game passes through our server.

Public quizzes

If you ask for a quiz to be made public, your device sends the whole quiz — title, description, tags, questions, answers and photos — to our server as a single file, and it waits in a queue until we have read it. Nothing is listed or playable by anyone else until we approve it. Your own copy stays on your device the whole time, and you can keep hosting games with it while it waits.

  • While it waits, we hold that file so that we can read it. If we turn the quiz down, we keep a short note of why — which only your device is shown — and delete the file. You can withdraw it at any time, which deletes it.
  • Once approved, the quiz is stored on our server and anyone using the service can browse, play and download it. Please don't publish anything you wouldn't want the public to see.
  • A published quiz is linked only to a one-way hash of your device's publisher key — not to your name, email, IP address or any account.
  • The app removes photo metadata (such as camera details and GPS location) on your device before a photo leaves it.
  • Editing a public quiz sends the change back through the same queue. The version already published stays as it is until we have read the change.
  • You can unpublish a quiz at any time from the device that published it. Its photos are then deleted automatically within about a day.
  • If you've lost that device and want a quiz you published removed, email us and tell us which quiz it is.
  • We may remove published quizzes that break the law or are abusive.

Reporting a quiz

Any public quiz can be reported from inside the app. We store what you chose as the reason, anything you typed in the box, and a one-way hash of your device's publisher key. The hash is there only so that one device counts as one report; it does not tell us who you are, and it is never shown to the person who published the quiz.

  • We read every report and decide within about a day whether to remove the quiz.
  • Reports are visible only to us. If we remove the quiz, its reports are deleted with it.
  • Don't put anything in the box you wouldn't want us to read.

Technical data

  • IP addresses. To protect the service from abuse, the server counts requests per IP address over short windows (about a minute). These counters live in memory only and are never written to disk.
  • Server logs. Like any web server, ours keeps logs of requests (time, address requested, and whether it succeeded) to diagnose problems and keep the service secure. We use them only for that, and don't combine them with anything else to identify people.
  • Cookies. The service sets no cookies for players or visitors. The only cookie is a session cookie for our own administration dashboard.

Photos and permissions (Android)

The app asks for only one permission: internet access. When you add a photo to a quiz, you pick it using Android's own photo picker, and the app only ever sees the photo you chose.

Third parties

We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and there is no advertising or analytics code in the app. A few parts of the service involve another company's servers, which may see your IP address when they're used:

  • GitHub — an app installed from our website checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from there. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Play — if you installed the app from Google Play, Google handles installs and updates under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — the web app bundles its own fonts, but if a quiz contains characters those fonts don't cover (such as some emoji or scripts), your browser may fetch a fallback font from Google's font servers.

Children

The service doesn't ask anyone for personal information, and it is not directed at children under 13. Published quizzes are visible to everyone, so children should publish only with a parent's help.

Your rights

Because we don't collect names, emails or accounts, we usually have nothing that identifies you. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU and UK), you have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Email contact@anisabdellatif.me and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

All connections to the server are encrypted with HTTPS. Publisher keys are stored only as hashes, so even we can't use them to act on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we'll say so in the app's release notes.
