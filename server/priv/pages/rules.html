Community Rules

Fazoura Party · Last updated 24 September 2026

The short version. Fazoura Party is for people aged 13 and over. Be the kind of player people want back: no hate, no harassment, nothing sexual, nothing violent or illegal, and nobody's personal details. These rules apply to everything you write that someone else sees — quizzes you publish, the name you play under, and the answers you give. You agree to them before you publish a quiz or play in a public room.

Who can play

Fazoura Party is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please don't use it.

What is not okay

Not in a quiz, a question, a photo, a display name or an answer:

  • Hate and harassment — attacking or demeaning people for who they are (race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, sex, gender, sexual orientation), slurs, bullying, or targeting a real person.
  • Sexual content — sexual or pornographic text or images, and anything sexualising minors, ever.
  • Violence and danger — threats, glorifying violence or terrorism, graphic gore, or encouraging self-harm or dangerous acts.
  • Illegal content — anything illegal, including promoting illegal drugs or weapons, and content you have no right to share.
  • Personal information — nobody's real address, phone number, school, email or other details, yours included. Play under a nickname.
  • Spam and cheating — advertising, links, nonsense quizzes, pretending to be someone else, or anything meant to wreck other people's game.

Public rooms

A public room is one anybody can find and join, so you will be playing with strangers. Names and answers in public rooms go through a word filter: a name it catches can't be used there, and an answer it catches is hidden from the other players. The filter only catches the obvious — the rules above apply to everything, filtered or not.

Never share personal details with people you meet in a room. If someone makes you uncomfortable, leave the room and report them.

Publishing quizzes

Every quiz sent for publication is read by a person before anyone else can see it, and one that breaks these rules is turned down. Only publish questions and photos you have the right to share.

What happens if someone breaks them

  • The host of a room can remove any player from it straight away.
  • Anyone can report a quiz or a player from inside the app. We read reports and answer them, usually within a day.
  • We can take a quiz down, end a room, or keep a connection out of public rooms for up to 90 days. How we do that without accounts is explained in the privacy policy.
